Bear Home Assessment™

A practical 45-minute assessment focused on what matters most.

This is not a full real estate inspection. It is a homeowner-focused assessment designed to understand the home, identify priorities, and build the first BearTrack™ profile.

Safety & readinessWater shutoff, electrical disconnect, smoke/CO alarms, trip hazards.
Weather protectionRoof from ground, exterior walls, windows, doors, caulking, drainage.
Major systemsPlumbing basics, HVAC basics, water heater, visible electrical, homeowner concerns.
Required focusEvery house should have an accessible water shutoff valve, and the homeowner should know where it is.
