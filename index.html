Florida service compliance

Clear lines between Bear Maintenance work and licensed partner work.

Bear Maintenance is designed to handle routine maintenance, small repairs, work orders, inspections, documentation, and homeowner support. When a task requires a licensed trade contractor, permit, or specialty certification, the work is routed through the Bear Partner Network and documented inside BearTrack™.

Important: This is a planning guide for Clay, Duval, and St. Johns County operations. Final service rules should be verified with county permitting offices and a Florida construction/licensing professional before launch.

Bear Maintenance

Typically suitable for our in-house Home Maintenance Specialists when no permit or licensed trade work is required.

  • Full-home inspections and BearTrack documentation
  • Work orders, honey-do lists, TV mounting, shelving, and furniture assembly
  • Caulking, weatherstripping, minor drywall, trim, doors, screens, and hardware
  • Filters, smoke detector batteries, basic maintenance, wellness checks, and punch-list work

Verify Locally

These may be allowed in limited situations, but should be checked against county rules, permitting requirements, and insurance limitations.

  • Replacing existing light fixtures, ceiling fans, switches, receptacles, or GFCIs
  • Replacing faucets, P-traps, toilet fill valves, flappers, and supply lines
  • Garbage disposals, appliance connections, thermostat swaps, and similar fixture replacements
  • Any task where wiring, plumbing, or equipment conditions are unclear

Licensed Partner

Major repairs and regulated trade work should be performed by properly licensed and insured partners, with Bear Maintenance providing oversight.

  • Roof replacement, structural work, window replacement, and major waterproofing repairs
  • HVAC system replacement, refrigerant work, compressors, and permitted HVAC repairs
  • Electrical panels, new circuits, rewiring, generators, and service upgrades
  • Water heaters, re-pipes, sewer lines, major plumbing changes, and slab/underground work

Multi-home & family plans

One trusted team for up to three homes — each home gets the tier it actually needs.

Multi-home owners should not be forced into one oversized plan. Each property receives its own BearTrack™ profile and its own 2, 4, or 6-hour membership recommendation. Additional homes on the same family account receive a $50 monthly discount per home.
